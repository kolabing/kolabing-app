# Kolabing 1.5.0 — store "What's New"

> Paste into App Store Connect (What's New) + Play Console (Release notes).
> Keep under ~500 chars per store field. EN below; ES/CA optional.

## English
Meet the new Kolabing.

• New attendee experience — quick onboarding, your own @handle, and a redesigned
  social profile you edit right in place (tap your photo to change it).
• Friends — add people by email or @handle.
• Discover events in your city, filter by date (today, this week, weekend, this
  month) and type.
• Communities can now publish events as Public, Members-only, or tier-specific —
  public events appear in your city's discovery. Pick a venue and the city is
  detected automatically.
• A fresh new look and app icon.

Plus reliability and performance fixes.
